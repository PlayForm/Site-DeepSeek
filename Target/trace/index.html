Lane

The same turn as time: who was busy, when, and for how long. A bar is one span of work, and its fill weight says what kind of work it was, never a hue.

The log carries a wall clock per record, so a lane is derivable from any log; the fixture carries no clock, so these spans are authored from the documented turn. The short bars on the record lane are the ledger writes, and the board names the files they land in.

The waterfalls are dispatched in memory and never written to the log: agent/pre-step, agent/request, llm/stream, and the three tools/* events. A durable trace can show that a call happened; it cannot show what a listener decided on the way through. The Claude and Codex hook bridge records its own decisions as hook/invoked and hook/result; the harness's own waterfalls do not.

Live

This page is static. A running harness would fill the same machines from a stream, and would not fill one dash in the coverage above.

A live source would change what fills these machines, not their shape: a tail that appends while the reader reads, an inspector that follows the newest record, a lane from the log's own clock, and a graph cut to the packages the session loaded.

The limit

Every dash above is a fact the harness decides and does not write. A live view of a log that lacks a fact is a live view of the same gap: the frames would arrive, and the decision behind them would still be missing.
