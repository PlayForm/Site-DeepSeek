Transform

What the model emits on the left, what the transcript stores on the right.

  1. normalize-dash U+2014 — → - the dash family: em, en, and seventeen exotic relatives
  2. normalize-ellipsis U+2026 … → ... the single ellipsis character, default replacement
  3. normalize-quotes U+201C “ → " eight typographic quotes to their straight ASCII counterpart
  4. normalize-spaces U+00A0   → exotic spaces to the plain ASCII space
  5. normalize-fullwidth U+FF21 Ａ → A the fullwidth ASCII block back to halfwidth
  6. normalize-invisible U+200B ​ → removed zero-width, joiner, bidi, and BOM characters removed
