The turn

The harness reaches 12 stages and 20 seams before a plugin of this family exists.

The claim

The default turn already has every slot. Only the slots are empty.

The family documents 7 of the 20 seams on this path and registers arms at 2 more, so the lit stages below are where it actually listens. Every other page on this site is a comparison against this one.

Power

A plugin acts at a seam, and the power the seam gives a listener decides what it can do there.

  • Change: the listener receives the payload and returns it changed, so the next reader sees the new value.
  • Observe: the listener sees a fact that already happened, and it can record or act beside it, but never rewrite it.
  • Stop: the listener may end the turn at the terminal checkpoint, but the turn it ends is already written.
