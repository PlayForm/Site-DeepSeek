Axis

The eight axes a reader actually turns, across the family. The exact field and default live on each package's page; this is the shape of the choice.

  • enabled 01 on: the listener stops returning the payload untouched
  • threshold 02 a smaller number: the hook acts sooner and on more results
  • replacement 03 another character or string: the normalizer writes what you name
The eight configuration axes, what each ships as, and what turning it does
