The same-observed-version guard race

LIVE-FOUND · FIXED

In the multi-step direct-govern fold every step wrote with the SAME observed version - only the first write could succeed, the later steps failed the stale-version check silently.

Solved: the sequential fold awaits each step's chain, and the chain pass stats the target before its write (the fresh-version pattern) - the fold converges in any step order.

The architecture in action

HOOKS · DELEGATIONS · INVERSIONS

The three attachment points: the llm/stream waterfall wrap (next() first, options untouched, per-chunk), the fs/observed listeners (sync, after content is on disk, fired by the tool layer only), and the tool registrations into the agent's toolset (raw-write, normalize-file).

Hooks - where the family attaches

The llm/stream waterfall wrap - next() first, options untouched, per-chunk.

The fs/observed listeners - sync, after content is on disk, fired by the tool layer only.

And the tool registrations into the agent's toolset: raw-write, normalize-file.

The work splitting across the family: the factory's Direct-govern steps (per basename, the raw-write govern selection), the core's Dispatch/Settle envelope builder (a new update stage reducing to a child runner plus strings), the subprocess seam for the external tools, and the Effect-TS services mirroring the same seams.

Delegations - who does the work

The factory's Direct-govern steps (per basename, the raw-write govern selection) and the core's Dispatch/Settle envelope builder - a new update stage reduces to a child runner plus strings.

The subprocess seam carries the external tools, and the Effect-TS services mirror the same seams.

The ownership boundaries: the Dependencies injection builders (every collection, child stage and string injected), the module-owned ledger strings (the byte-identical contract), the consumers bringing their own metal while the factory pours the mold, and the smokes as the arbiter after every change.
