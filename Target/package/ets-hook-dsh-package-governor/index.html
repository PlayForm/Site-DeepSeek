Flow

The order this package's work happens in, with the branch that decides it inline, transcribed from the source; the rule it obeys is the last band of Architecture.

  1. 01
    1. seam fs/observed
    why

    A tool-layer write lands, carrying its path, its actor, and its version token.

  2. 02
    Gate: the basename package.json, a mutation tool, a present observation, the stash, and the exclusion list
    why

    The gate writes nothing to the ledger; the module composes its own lines.

  3. 03
    fail skipped (excluded) <path>
    why

    Exclusion-first: the internals of a dependency are never governed.

  4. 04
    Discover the nearest registry.json, and parse it
    why

    The walk-up is an auxiliary plain-fs probe; the governed manifest is never read here.

  5. 05
    branch No registry.json found, or it is unreadable
    why

    The chain pass is skipped, and the line says which of the two happened.

  6. 06
    Chain pass: canonicalize every chain-governed pin to the resolved range
    why

    A dependency outside the chain is left where the author put it.

  7. 07
    branch The refusal guard: a non-dependency section would change
    why

    The rewrite is aborted, and the refusal names the section.

  8. 08
    GuardedWrite replaceIfVersion, then re-emit fs/observed with the fresh version
    why

    The author sees its own bytes; the observation policy's version record is refreshed.

  9. 09
    Update stage: npm-check-updates in process, or the ncu bin
    why

    A detached continuation off the settled chain pass, with the chain deps rejected.

  10. 10
    branch The circuit breaker: maxUpdateFailures in a row for one directory
    why

    The directory pauses, and the line carries the count.

  11. 11
    store ~/.dsh/playform/ets-hook-dsh-package-governor.log
    why

    One global ledger, with the module identity as the line prefix.

Harness

The laws the package states, the line it writes when it loads, the seam the harness dispatches with none of the family, and the surface and knobs a run can set.

Scheme

The laws the package states in its own SCHEME.md: when it runs, what it refuses, and what stays identical because of it.

01 P The chain pass: decode, satisfy the registry, and rewrite a chain-governed pin. Pure, deterministic, idempotent.
02 U The update stage: policy-gated, cooldown-guarded, async, and skipped when the chain pass changed nothing.
Trigger The governor fires only on an author write to a package.json.
  • observation.kind is present
  • actor.name is write, edit, or str_replace_editor
  • basename(displayPath) is package.json
Exclusion A path inside the tooling is never governed.
  • no path segment is node_modules, .git, .dsh, .pnpm, .store
  • no path segment is the harness application bundle
Non-trigger The governor does not trigger itself.
  • its own write goes through the fs service, which dispatches no fs/observed
  • its update stage is a continuation, not an author call
Identical with and without
  • the tool result the author receives
  • the transcript the model reads
  • the session events the log records
Different, and only here
  • the bytes on disk, discoverable only by a later read
  • the ledger line the module writes

Default

What the harness does at this package's seam with none of the family installed, so the surface below has something to be a difference from.

The harness dispatches fs/observed as a emit and already has listeners there: fs-observation-policy, skill-filesystem, workspace-files.

An emit listener observes a fact that already happened, so the harness's own listeners can record it and cannot change it. This package records beside them, or acts on the world rather than on the payload.

Architecture

This plugin's own structure: the shape the portrait gives it, the facts its source documents, and the rule its flow obeys.

Shape

Plugs into the fs/observed event through the factory's Wire, and the direct-govern entries for package.json.

The silent governor of every written package.json.

  • Gates on the basename, the actor tool, a present observation, the stash, and the exclusion list
  • Canonicalizes a chain-governed pin to the resolved range the nearest registry records
  • Bumps the public pins from a detached update stage, in process or through the ncu bin
  • Re-emits fs/observed with the fresh version, so the observation policy's record is current

Its shape machine belongs to Person.

Rule

The manifest is the only thing that changes: the listener never awaits, the chain pass and the update stage run as detached continuations, and the author is never told.
