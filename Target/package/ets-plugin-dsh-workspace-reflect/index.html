Architecture

This plugin's own structure: the shape the portrait gives it, the facts its source documents, and the rule its flow obeys.

Shape

Plugs into the ctx.workspaceReflect service, the policy's write verdict, and the factory's guarded write.

The one-call reflective operations: a rename, a connect, or a replace over the managed representations.

  • A rename through the semantics plan, else the whole-word engine
  • A connect that refuses a connection already there
  • A replace after the fingerprint check, inside the block bound
  • A reply union, never a boolean or a throw
Service ctx.workspaceReflect
4 Methods
3 Consumers
Methods
  1. rename the AST-aware rename, through the semantics plan first
  2. connect join two files with an import or a reference comment
  3. replace replace a node's span, or a plain span, after the fingerprint check
  4. act the dispatch by the operation's kind
Consumed by
  • the operate step, which proposes an operation
  • the graph, which re-indexes the file after an apply
  • the semantics service, which supplies the rename plan

Rule The reply is part of the type: every operation answers applied, conflict, denied, rejected, recovery-required, or outcome-unknown, never a boolean and never a thrown error.

Rule

The policy is the outer check and the fingerprint the inner one, and the reply is part of the type rather than a boolean or a throw.
